# 🚀 What's New in QuickBiz for Windows 1.1.0

QuickBiz on Windows just got a big update. Enjoy a fresh new look, more control over your business, and easier ways to manage sales and payments. 💙

### ✨ A Fresh New Look

QuickBiz now feels more familiar on Windows, with a design inspired by the mobile app.

- A new side menu with icons. Collapse it when you need more screen space.
- A redesigned dashboard with your balance, quick actions and this month's spending.
- A cleaner sign-in screen, with a **Forgot password?** option and the ability to show your password.

### 👤 Manage Your Profile on Windows

You can now manage more of your account without reaching for your phone.

- Update your name, profile picture and currency.
- Switch between Personal and Business mode, or create a business.
- Change your business name, category and logo.
- Invite employees and manage what they can access.
- Send us questions or suggestions and read our replies.

### 🏪 Sales That Fit Your Business

Different businesses handle sales differently. Now, QuickBiz gives you a sales option that matches your business:

- **Shops:** Record credit sales when customers pay later.
- **Restaurants:** Manage orders.
- **Service businesses:** Keep track of jobs.
- **Mobile repair shops:** Manage repair jobs.
- **Other businesses:** Record amounts customers still owe you.

### 💰 Keep Track of Customer Payments

It's easier to know how much money you're still waiting to collect.

- Click **Record Payment** on an entry with money due when a customer pays.
- The new **Customers owe you** card on your dashboard shows how much is still outstanding.

**QuickBiz for Windows 1.1.0 is now available.** 💙
