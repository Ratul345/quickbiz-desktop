## Description
QuickBiz helps you keep track of your business money, stock and daily sales in one place, on your Windows PC.

Record every sale and every cost, keep an eye on what is in stock, and see how the month is going at a glance. Everything is saved to your QuickBiz account, so what you add on your PC also shows up on your phone, and the other way around.

Built for small shops and service businesses:

• Dashboard: your balance, this month's income and expenses, what customers still owe you, and which products are running low.
• Finance: add income and expenses in seconds, search your history, and remove a mistaken entry.
• Repair and service jobs: write down the customer, the problem, the total price and what has been paid, and see what is still due.
• Inventory: keep your product list with prices and stock, record stock purchases, returns and adjustments, and see the stock history of each product.
• Reports: income and expenses for the last 7 days, 30 days or 12 months, with a clear chart and a breakdown of where the money went.
• Business and Personal modes: keep your shop's books apart from your own spending.
• Many currencies: Taka, US dollar, euro, pound, rupee, Australian dollar and Canadian dollar.

It opens fast, uses little memory and works with the same QuickBiz account you use on Android. Sign in, and your data is there.

QuickBiz is free to use. An internet connection is needed to sign in and to save your records.

## What's new in this version
A fresh new look, your full profile, and sales that fit your kind of business.

• New look: matches the QuickBiz phone app, with a side menu you can fold to icons only, a new dashboard and a new sign-in screen.
• Your profile on Windows: change your name, photo and currency, switch between Personal and Business, edit your business, invite employees and choose what they can do, and contact support.
• Made for your business: Credit sale for shops, Order for restaurants, Job for services, Repair for mobile repair shops, and Customer owes for everyone else.
• Collect what you're owed: record a payment when a customer pays later, and see who still owes you on the dashboard.
